# Nermine Bouabid

Personal academic website of Nermine Bouabid, final-year PhD candidate in Accounting and Finance at Lancaster University Management School and Affiliated Researcher at the Pentland Centre for Sustainability in Business. I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

My research studies the information environment around firms' relationship with nature: what can be learned about firms by looking beyond what they disclose, through satellite imagery of their facilities, the language of their sustainability reports, and the intermediaries that carry information about them.

The site includes my research (including my job market paper, *Physical Peers: A Satellite-Based Measure of Firm Similarity*), talks, code, teaching, honors and memberships.
